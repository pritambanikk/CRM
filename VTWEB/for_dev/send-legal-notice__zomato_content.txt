[When Can You Send a Legal Notice to Zomato?]
    You can send Zomato a legal notice when their support has failed to
    resolve your complaint. Common situations include:
    Food ordered but marked delivered without actual delivery
    Wrong order delivered and refund denied
    Food arrived in dangerously poor condition — spoiled, foreign objects found
    Delivery executive misbehaviour or fraud
    Zomato Pro or Gold membership benefits not honoured
    Platform fee or surge charges added without disclosure
    Cancelled order with no refund to original payment method
    Restaurant listed on Zomato that is permanently closed or unlicensed

[Step-by-Step: How to Escalate Your Zomato Complaint]
    Step 1 — Report in the Zomato App
    Use the Help section in the Zomato app immediately after the issue.
    Screenshot all chat responses and note the complaint ticket ID.
    Step 2 — Escalate to Zomato Grievance Officer
    Email grievance.officer@zomato.com with your order ID, issue description,
    and previous complaint reference. Zomato must respond within 15 days.
    Step 3 — File on National Consumer Helpline
    Call 1915 or visit consumerhelpline.gov.in if Zomato does not resolve
    within 15 days of the grievance email.
    Step 4 — Send a Legal Notice
    If the above steps fail, send a formal legal notice through a licensed
    advocate. Even for small amounts, companies like Zomato respond quickly
    to notices from advocates to avoid consumer court records.
    Step 5 — File in Consumer Court
    If Zomato ignores the legal notice within 15–30 days, file a consumer
    complaint in the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission in
    your city. For food safety issues, also file with FSSAI.

    All previous complaint attempts with dates and ticket numbers
    Exact refund or compensation amount claimed
    Legal basis — Consumer Protection Act 2019, Food Safety and Standards
    Act 2006 (if food quality is the issue), IT Act 2000
    Deadline — 15 days from receipt
    Consequence — filing in District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission
    and/or FSSAI complaint
    Send via Speed Post with Acknowledgment Due only.

[Which Laws Protect You Against Zomato?]
  ▸ Consumer Protection Act, 2019
    Zomato as a platform is responsible for ensuring restaurants meet quality
    and delivery standards. Non-delivery, refund denial, and unfair platform
    fees all constitute deficiency in service.
  ▸ Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (FSSAI)
    If your complaint involves food quality — spoiled food, foreign objects,
    or hygiene violations — you can simultaneously file an FSSAI complaint
    against the restaurant. Zomato is responsible for listing only FSSAI-licensed
    restaurants.
  ▸ Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
    Zomato must maintain a clear grievance mechanism and cannot engage in
    misleading pricing or undisclosed charges.
  ▸ Information Technology Act, 2000
    Zomato must appoint a grievance officer who responds within 15 days.
    Failure to do so is a violation of the IT Act.

[Frequently Asked Questions]

  ▸ What is Zomato's registered address for sending a legal notice?
    Send your notice to Zomato Limited (CIN: L93030DL2010PLC198141),
    Ground Floor 12A, 94 Meghdoot, Nehru Place, New Delhi – 110019.
    Grievance email: grievance.officer@zomato.com.

  ▸ My Zomato order amount is small — is it worth sending a legal notice?
    Yes. Consumer courts in India handle claims of any amount. More
    importantly, a legal notice from an advocate often prompts Zomato to
    settle immediately to avoid legal costs and reputational damage — even
    for orders of a few hundred rupees.

  ▸ Can I complain about food quality through a legal notice?
    Yes. Poor food quality, contaminated food, or food that caused illness
    is both a consumer complaint and a food safety violation. Your notice
    can cite the Consumer Protection Act 2019 and you can simultaneously
    file an FSSAI complaint at fssai.gov.in.

  ▸ Zomato says the restaurant is responsible, not them. Is that correct?
    Partially. While the restaurant prepares the food, Zomato is jointly
    responsible as the platform under consumer protection law — especially
    for non-delivery, payment issues, and for listing restaurants that
    violate food safety standards.

  ▸ How long does Zomato have to respond to a legal notice?
    The notice gives Zomato 15 days to respond. If there is no satisfactory
    response, you can file a consumer complaint in your city's District
    Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

  ▸ Can I file in my city even though Zomato is registered in Delhi?
    Yes. Under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, you can file a complaint
    where you reside or where the transaction took place. You do not need
    to travel to Delhi.
