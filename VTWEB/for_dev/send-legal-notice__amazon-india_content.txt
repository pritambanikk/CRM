[IMPORTANT — Send Your Notice to the Correct Amazon Entity]
    Amazon operates in India through multiple legal entities. The correct entity
    for consumer complaints related to purchases on Amazon.in is Amazon Seller
    Services Private Limited — this is the company that operates the Amazon.in
    marketplace, processes your orders, and is legally responsible for consumer
    transactions.
    Do not confuse it with Amazon Web Services India (cloud services) or Amazon
    Pay India (payment wallet) — those are separate entities for different
    services. For shopping complaints on Amazon.in, always address your notice
    to Amazon Seller Services Private Limited.

[When Can You Send a Legal Notice to Amazon India?]
    You can send a legal notice to Amazon when customer support has failed.
    Common qualifying situations include:
    Refund not processed despite the return being picked up or approved
    Product received was fake, counterfeit, or significantly not as described
    Order marked as delivered but never received
    Amazon's A-to-Z Guarantee claim was denied unfairly
    Third-party seller fraud where Amazon refuses to take responsibility

    Unauthorised charges or double payments on your account
    Amazon Prime benefits not delivered as promised

[Step-by-Step: How to Escalate Your Amazon Complaint]
    Step 1 — Contact Amazon Customer Support
    Use the Amazon app or website. Always note your case ID and screenshot all
    conversations. Request email confirmation of every response.
    Step 2 — Escalate to Amazon Grievance Officer
    Email grievance-officer@amazon.in with your order ID, case IDs, and the
    specific resolution you are seeking. Amazon is legally required to respond
    within 15 days under the IT Act, 2000.
    Step 3 — File on National Consumer Helpline
    Call 1915 or file online at consumerhelpline.gov.in if the Grievance
    Officer does not resolve within 15 days.
    Step 4 — Send a Legal Notice
    If all above steps fail, send a formal legal notice through a licensed
    advocate. This step prompts Amazon's legal team to review your case
    personally and often leads to swift resolution.
    Step 5 — File in Consumer Court
    If Amazon does not respond within 15–30 days of the legal notice, file
    a consumer complaint in the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission
    in your city.

[Which Laws Protect You Against Amazon India?]
  ▸ Consumer Protection Act, 2019
    Amazon as a marketplace is responsible for ensuring seller accountability.
    Refund denials, fake products, and non-delivery are deficiency in service
    and unfair trade practices under this Act.
  ▸ Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
    Amazon must provide clear grievance mechanisms, display seller information,
    and not engage in price manipulation. Violations are reported to the CCPA.
  ▸ Indian Contract Act, 1872
    Amazon's acceptance of your order and payment forms a contract. Failure
    to deliver or refund is a breach of contract entitling you to claim the
    amount with interest.
  ▸ Information Technology Act, 2000
    Amazon must appoint and maintain a grievance officer who responds within
    15 days. Failure to respond is itself a violation.

[Frequently Asked Questions]

  ▸ What is Amazon India's registered address for a legal notice?
    Send your notice to Amazon Seller Services Private Limited, 8th Floor,
    Brigade Gateway, 26/1 Dr. Rajkumar Road, Bangalore, Karnataka – 560055.
    The Grievance Officer is at International Trade Tower, Nehru Place,
    New Delhi – 110019. Grievance email: grievance-officer@amazon.in.

  ▸ Is Amazon responsible for third-party seller fraud on its platform?
    Yes. Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020, Amazon as a
    marketplace is responsible for seller accountability. You can send your
    notice to both the seller and Amazon Seller Services Private Limited.

  ▸ Amazon denied my A-to-Z Guarantee claim. What can I do?
    An unjust A-to-Z denial is a deficiency in service under the Consumer
    Protection Act 2019. You can challenge this through a legal notice and
    subsequently at the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

  ▸ How long does Amazon have to respond to a legal notice?
    The notice gives Amazon 15 to 30 days to respond. If they do not respond
    satisfactorily, you can immediately file a consumer complaint in your
    nearest District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

  ▸ Can I file a consumer complaint in my city even though Amazon is in Bangalore?
    Yes. Under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, you can file a complaint in
    the district where you reside or where the transaction took place. You do
    not need to go to Bangalore.

  ▸ What is the time limit to file a complaint against Amazon?
    2 years from the date the cause of action arose under the Consumer
    Protection Act 2019. Act before this window closes.
