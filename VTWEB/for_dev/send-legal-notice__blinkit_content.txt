[Step-by-Step: How to Escalate Your Blinkit Complaint]
    Step 1 — Contact Blinkit Customer Support
    Use the official app or website. Note all ticket IDs and screenshot
    all conversations and chat responses.
    Step 2 — Escalate to Blinkit Grievance Officer
    Email the grievance officer with full complaint details and all previous
    reference numbers. They must respond within 15 days under IT Act 2000.
    Step 3 — National Consumer Helpline
    Call 1915 or file at consumerhelpline.gov.in if unresolved in 15 days.
    Step 4 — Send a Legal Notice
    Send a formal legal notice through a licensed advocate. This escalates
    the matter to Blinkit's legal team directly.
    Step 5 — Consumer Court
    File in the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission in your
    city if the legal notice is ignored within 15–30 days.

[Which Laws Protect You Against Blinkit?]
    Consumer Protection Act 2019, Food Safety and Standards Act 2006, Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020
    Consumer Protection Act, 2019 — your primary protection as a consumer.
    IT Act, 2000 — requires a grievance officer who must respond in 15 days.
    Indian Contract Act, 1872 — failure to deliver agreed service is breach.
