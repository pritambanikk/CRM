[Which Laws Protect You Against Meesho?]
  ▸ Consumer Protection Act, 2019
    Meesho as a marketplace is responsible for seller accountability.
    Fake products, non-delivery, and refund refusals are deficiency
    in service and unfair trade practices.
  ▸ Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
    Meesho must ensure sellers provide accurate product information.
    Misleading listings are a direct violation.
  ▸ Legal Metrology Act, 2009
    If the product you received has incorrect weight, size, or packaging
    information, this is also a violation you can include in your notice.
  ▸ Indian Contract Act, 1872
    Meesho's acceptance of your order forms a contract to deliver what
    was described. Delivering a different product is breach of contract.
