[Step-by-Step: How to Escalate Your Swiggy Complaint]
    Step 1 — Report Immediately in the Swiggy App
    Use the Help section in the Swiggy app right after the issue. Report
    within 24 hours for best results. Screenshot all chat responses and
    note your complaint ticket ID.
    Step 2 — Escalate to Swiggy Grievance Officer
    Email grievances@swiggy.in with your order ID, complaint ticket, and
    the specific resolution you are seeking. Swiggy must respond within 15
    days under the IT Act, 2000.
    Step 3 — National Consumer Helpline
    Call 1915 or file at consumerhelpline.gov.in if Swiggy does not
    respond within 15 days.
    Step 4 — Send a Legal Notice
    Send a formal legal notice through a licensed advocate. This step
    typically results in Swiggy's executive resolution team reviewing
    your case directly, bypassing frontline support.
    Step 5 — Consumer Court
    If Swiggy ignores the legal notice within 15–30 days, file at the
    District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission in your city.

[Which Laws Protect You Against Swiggy?]
  ▸ Consumer Protection Act, 2019
    Swiggy is responsible for ensuring restaurants meet quality and delivery
    standards on its platform. Non-delivery, refund denial, and unfair charges
    are deficiency in service under this Act.
  ▸ Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (FSSAI)
    Swiggy must list only FSSAI-licensed restaurants. If you received unsafe
    or adulterated food, file simultaneously with your State's FSSAI office.
  ▸ Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
    Swiggy must maintain a transparent grievance mechanism and cannot
    engage in undisclosed charges or misleading listings.
  ▸ Information Technology Act, 2000
    Swiggy must appoint a grievance officer who responds within 15 days.

[Frequently Asked Questions]

  ▸ What is Swiggy's registered address for a legal notice?
    Send your notice to Swiggy Limited (CIN: L74110KA2013PLC096530),
    No. 55, Sy No. 8-14, Ground Floor, I&J Block, Embassy Tech Village,
    Outer Ring Road, Devarbisanahalli, Bengaluru – 560103, Karnataka.
    Grievance email: grievances@swiggy.in.

  ▸ Swiggy credited my refund to Swiggy wallet instead of my bank. Is that legal?
    No — if you specifically requested a bank refund, crediting it to Swiggy
    wallet without consent is an unfair trade practice under the Consumer
    Protection Act 2019. You can demand a bank refund through a legal notice.

  ▸ My order amount is small. Is a legal notice practical?
    Yes. A legal notice often costs less than the dispute amount but prompts
    immediate resolution. Many Swiggy disputes under ₹5,000 are resolved
    within days of the notice being received.

  ▸ Swiggy says the restaurant is at fault. What do I do?
    Swiggy is jointly liable for platform failures including non-delivery,
    payment issues, and listing non-compliant restaurants. Your legal notice
    should name Swiggy Limited as the primary respondent.

  ▸ How long does Swiggy have to respond to a legal notice?
    15 to 30 days as specified in the notice. After that you can proceed
    to consumer court in your city.

  ▸ Can I file in my city even though Swiggy is in Bengaluru?
    Yes. You can file where you reside or where the transaction took place
    under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.
